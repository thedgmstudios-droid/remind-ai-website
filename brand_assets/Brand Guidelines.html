05 — Shadow & Elevation

Shadow Usage

Shadows are used sparingly — only the FAB gets an explicit shadow to establish it as a floating action layer above the list.

09 — Voice & Tone

Writing Principles

✓ Do

Use the thing as the reminder title — "Call Mom", "Water the plants", "Submit report".

✗ Don't

Use the command as the title — "Set a reminder to call Mom", "Remind me to water the plants".

✓ Do

Empty states are short, specific, and action-oriented: "No reminders yet · Tap + or open Chat."

✗ Don't

Use marketing fluff in product copy: "Start your journey to a more organized life!"

✓ Do

AI confirmations are terse and informative: "Got it. I'll remind you to [title] on [date]."

✗ Don't

Over-explain in chat replies or add unnecessary filler: "Sure! I've gone ahead and created a reminder…"

Navigation Labels

Tab titles: Reminders · Chat · People. Sheet titles: New reminder · Edit reminder. Sheet actions: Save / Cancel. Swipe actions: Done · Undo · Skip · Delete. All labels are title case. Section headers are uppercase.
